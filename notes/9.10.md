## 二、教育与思维发展话题 (Education & Mental Development)

* 正规教育限制了我们的思想与精神：formal education limits our minds / spirits
* 死板僵化的教学法：rigid teaching
* 从根本上：fundamentally
* 基础知识与社会意识：basic knowledge and social awareness
* 系统性事实、规则与逻辑工具：systematic facts / rules / logical tools
* 通过提供系统性知识和逻辑工具来解放思想：frees minds by providing systematic knowledge and logical tools
* 迷信：superstitions
* 个人感受 / 主观情绪：personal feelings
* 科学与数学：理性地质疑事物：science / maths: question things reasonably
* 教导学生：teach students
* 科学教育教导学生科学方法与实证实验：science education teach students scientific method and empirical experiments
* 寻找新医疗方案或解决技术难题的突破，来自多年的基础正规训练而非纯粹的想象：breakthroughs in finding new medical treatments or solving tech problems come from years of basic formal training rather than pure imagination
* 因此，正规教育并没有限制思想；它赋予了人们探索、理解和改变世界所必需的工具：therefore, formal education does not limit thought; it gives people necessary tools to explore, understand and change the world
* 通过开阔视野与传授社会责任感来解放精神：free spirits by broadening our horizons and teaching social responsibility
* 个人经历通常受限于家庭与环境：personal experience is usually limited by family and environment
* 让学生接触历史、文学、艺术以及多元观念：expose students to history, literature, art and different attitudes
* 帮助学生理解他人的困境并培养同理心：help students understand other people's struggles and develop empathy
* 将人类精神从狭隘的自私自利提升至更高的公民使命：lift the human spirit from a narrow self-interest to a higher civic purpose
