
**The recommendation presents a view that I would agree is successful most of the time, but one that I cannot fully support due to the “all or nothing” impression it gives.**

> 该建议提出了一种观点，我在大多数情况下会承认它是成功的，但由于它给人一种“非黑即白（全盘接受或全盘否定）”的印象，我无法完全支持它。

**Certainly as an educator I agree fully that the best way to elicit positive response from students is to make use of students’ positive energy and then encourage actions that you would like to see repeated.**

> 作为一名教育工作者，我当然完全赞同：引发学生积极回应的最佳方法，是利用学生的正能量，然后鼓励那些你希望他们重复的行为。

**It is human nature that we all want to be accepted and achieve on some level, and when people in authority provide feedback that we have done something well, the drive to repeat the action that was praised is bound to be particularly strong.**

> 人性使然，我们都渴望在某种程度上获得接纳与成就；当权威人士反馈我们某事做得很好时，重复受表扬行为的驱动力必然会格外强烈。



**This blanket statement would obviously pay dividends in situations in which a teacher desires to have students repeat particular behaviors.**

> 在教师希望学生重复特定行为的情境下，这一一概而论的陈述显然会产生效果。

**For example, if an educator is attempting to teach students proper classroom etiquette, it would be appropriate to openly praise a student who raises his or her hand when wishing to speak or address the class.**

> 例如，如果一位教育工作者试图向学生传授正确的课堂礼仪，公开表扬那些在希望发言或对全班讲话时举手的学生将是恰当的。

**In such cases, the teacher may also help shape positive behaviors by ignoring a student who is trying to interject without approval from the teacher.**

> 在这种情况下，老师还可以通过忽视那些试图未经许可就插话的学生，来帮助塑造积极的行为。

**In fact, the decision to ignore students who are exhibiting inappropriate behaviors of this type could work very well in this situation, as the stakes are not very high and the intended outcome can likely be achieved by such a method.**

> 事实上，决定忽视表现出这类不当行为的学生在此情境下会非常有效，因为利害关系并不高，且通过这种方法很可能达到预期的结果。

**However, it is important to note here that this tactic would only be effective in such a “low-stakes” situation, as when a student speaks without raising her hand first.**

> 然而，这里需要指出的是，这种策略仅在诸如学生未先举手就发言这种“低风险”情境下才有效。

**As we will discuss below, ignoring a student who hits another student, or engages in more serious misbehaviors, would not be effective or prudent.**

> 正如我们下面将要讨论的，忽视一个殴打他人或从事更严重不良行为的学生，既不会有效，也不明智。

---


**To expand on this point, it is important for teachers to be careful when working with the second half of this statement, only ignoring negative actions that are not serious.**

> 延伸这一观点，对教师而言，在处理这句话的后半部分——即“只忽视不严重的负面行为”时——保持谨慎至关重要。

**Take for instance a student who is misbehavior just by chatting with a fellow class-mate.**

> 以一个仅仅通过与同学闲聊而表现不佳的学生为例。

**This student might not be presenting much of a problem and may be simply seeking attention.**

> 这个学生可能并没有带来多大问题，或许只是在博取关注。

**Ignoring the student might, in fact, be the best solution.**

> 事实上，忽视这名学生可能是最佳的解决方案。

**Now assume the negative action is the improper administering of chemicals in a science experiment or the bullying of a fellow student.**

> 现在假设负面行为是在科学实验中违规操作化学品，或是霸凌同学。

**To ignore these negative actions would be absurd and negligent.**

> 忽视这些负面行为将是荒谬且玩忽职守的。

**Now you are allowing a problem to persist, one that could potentially lead to much bigger and more dangerous issues.**

> 此时，你是在纵容一个问题持续存在，而这个问题原本可能引发更大、更危险的后果。

**In a more serious situation, addressing the negative actions quickly and properly could stop the problem it in its tracks.**

> 在更严重的情况下，迅速而妥善地处理负面行为可以将其扼杀在萌芽状态。

**It is for reasons like this that I do not advocate the idea that a teacher can be successful by simply ignoring negative actions.**

> 正是因为诸如此类的原因，我不主张“教师只需简单忽视负面行为就能获得成功”这一观点。


**I do, however, greatly support the idea that the central focus of teaching should be to build on and encourage positive actions.**

> 然而，我非常支持这种观点：教学的核心重点应该是建立并鼓励积极的行为。

**However, the author’s all-encompasing statement leaves too many negative possibilities for the classroom.**

> 不过，作者这种包罗万象的陈述给课堂留下了太多负面的可能性。

**Perhaps a better way to phrase this statement would be to say, “The best way to teach is to praise positive actions and ignore negative ones that are not debilitating to class efficiency or the safety of any individual”.**

> 重新表述这句话的更好方式或许是：“最好的教学方法是表扬积极的行为，并忽视那些不会损害课堂效率或任何人安全的消极行为。”


**Thus, in the original statement, there are indeed some good intentions, and there could be a lot of merit in adopting its basic principles.**

> 因此，在最初的陈述中，确实包含了一些良好的意图，采纳其基本原则也可能具有很大的长处。

**Data proves that positive support can substantially increase motivation and desire in students and contribute to positive achievements.**

> 数据证明，积极的支持可以大幅提升学生的动机和渴望，并促成积极的成就。

**In fact, most studies of teaching efficacy indicate that praising positive actions and ignoring negative ones can create a more stable and efficient classroom.**

> 事实上，大多数关于教学效能的研究表明，表扬积极行为并忽视消极行为能够营造一个更稳定、更高效的课堂。

**It needs to be stressed, however, that this tool is only effective at certain levels of misbehavior.**

> 然而，需要强调的是，这一工具仅在特定程度的不良行为中才有效。

**As mentioned above, when the behavior is precipitated by feelings of revenge, power or total self-worthlessness, this methodology will likely not work.**

> 如前所述，当这种行为是由报复心理、权力欲或完全的自我价值缺失所引发时，这种方法很可能不管用。

**It is likely to be very successful, however, when the drive behind the misbehavior is simple attention seeking.**

> 然而，当不良行为背后的驱动力仅仅是博取关注时，它很可能会非常成功。

**In many of these instances, if the teacher demonstrates clearly that inappropriate behavior does not result in the gaining of attention, students are more likely to seek attention by behaving properly.**

> 在许多此类情况下，如果老师明确表明不当行为无法换取关注，学生就更有可能通过表现得体来获取关注。

**Should the student choose this path, then the ignoring has worked and when the positive behavior is exhibited, then the teacher can utilize the first part of the theory and support or praise this behavior.**

> 如果学生选择了这条路径，那么忽视就起到了效果；而当积极行为展现出来时，老师就可以运用该理论的第一部分，去支持或表扬这种行为。

**Now it is much more likely to be repeated.**

> 这样一来，该行为就更有可能被重复。

**If the student does not choose this path and instead elects to raise the actions to a higher level that presents a more serious issue, then ignorance alone cannot work and other methods must be employed.**

> 如果学生没有选择这条路径，而是选择将行为升级为更严重的问题，那么单靠忽视就行不通了，必须采用其他方法。




**In conclusion, one can appreciate the credo expressed in this instance, but surely we all can see the potential error of following it through to the extreme.**

> 总之，人们可以理解本例中所表达的信条，但我们大家肯定都能看出将其推向极端的潜在谬误。
