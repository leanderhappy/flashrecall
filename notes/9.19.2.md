(第二，企业必须保护环境，因为它们的污染会伤害整个社会。)


Big companies use huge amounts of natural resources. If they only care about saving costs, they will cut corners on safety. When an accident happens, innocent people and local businesses have to pay the price.
(大公司消耗大量的自然资源。如果他们只在乎节省成本，就会在安全上偷工减料。一旦发生事故，无辜的民众和当地企业就不得不为此买单。)


A famous example is the BP oil spill in 2010. To save time and money, the oil company BP cut safety corners on its drilling rig. This caused a massive explosion in the Gulf of Mexico. Millions of barrels of oil polluted the ocean, killing wildlife and destroying the lives of local fishermen. In the end, the company had to pay tens of billions of dollars to clean up the mess.
(一个著名的例子是 2010 年英国石油公司（BP）漏油事件。为了省时省钱，BP 公司在钻井平台上偷工减料。这导致墨西哥湾发生特大爆炸。数百万桶原油污染了海洋，杀死了野生动物，也毁了当地渔民的生计。最终，这家公司不得不支付数百亿美元来收拾残局。)
